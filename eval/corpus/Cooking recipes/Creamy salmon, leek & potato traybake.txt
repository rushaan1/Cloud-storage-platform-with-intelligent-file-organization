Creamy salmon, leek & potato traybake
Esther Clark
Serves 2 Easy
Prep: 5 mins Cook: 35 mins
Nestle leeks, potato and capers around salmon fillets to make
this easy traybake for two. It's great as an midweek meal, or for
a more romantic occasion
Egg-free Gluten-free High-protein Nut-free

Method
Step 1
Heat the oven to 200C/180C fan/gas 6. Bring a medium pan of water to the boil. Add the potatoes and cook for 8 mins. Drain
and leave to steam-dry in a colander for a few minutes. Toss the potatoes with ½ of the oil and plenty of seasoning in a baking
tray. Put in the oven for 20 mins, tossing halfway through the cooking time.
Step 2
Meanwhile, heat the remaining oil in a frying pan over a medium heat. Add the leek and fry for 5 mins, or until beginning to
soften. Stir through the garlic for 1 min, then add the cream, capers and 75ml hot water, then bring to the boil. Stir through the
chives.
Step 3
Heat the grill to high. Pour the creamy leek mixture over the potatoes, then sit the salmon fillets on top. Grill for 7-8 mins, or until
just cooked through. Serve topped with extra chives and capers and a salad on the side, if you like
